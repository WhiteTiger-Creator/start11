# ledgerstore 1.6

Released 2023-08-11.

## Collation

- **Key order is raw UTF-8 byte order.** Every ordered structure -- the base,
  segment min/max bounds, shard boundaries and range scans -- compares the
  encoded bytes of a key. The 1.5 behaviour took the order the client library
  reports, which compares UTF-16 code units. The two agree on everything inside
  the basic multilingual plane, so ASCII and most of our key space never showed
  it, but they reverse above it: a key whose first differing character is
  astral leads under UTF-16, because its lead surrogate sits below U+E000,
  while its UTF-8 encoding begins F0 and sorts after the EF of anything in
  U+F000..U+FFFF. The divergence showed up as scans that skipped keys near a
  shard boundary.
- Segment bounds recorded before 1.6 remain valid; they are recomputed on the
  next compaction.

## Fixed

- A range scan crossing a shard boundary could return a key twice.
- `ledgerctl bounds` printed the max key of the wrong segment at level 2.
